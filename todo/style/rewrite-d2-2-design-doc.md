# Rewrite the D2.2 design doc

[`docs/m2/d2.2/design.md`](/docs/m2/d2.2/design.md) has grown by accretion since the start of D2.2: dated notes at the top, results appended to the paragraphs that planned them, and plans for the grammar of `red` beside plans for the in-context grammar. It is now hard to follow, and its register predates the one the reports use now (plain wording, few numbers in prose, decisions made with the results in hand, no talk of numbered rounds).

A full rewrite rather than an edit. It should say where D2.2 stands (what the in-context grammar is, what ex-2.2.21 and ex-2.2.22 found, what is still to show for the deliverable), what comes next and why, and the open risks, and leave the history of how we got here to the reports and the index. Material that only records past decisions can move to the reports it came from, or go. Write it as a design doc a newcomer could read in one sitting, under the `writing` and `style-terms` skills.
