# Is the retention drift the model still reorganizing its representations? Training-dynamics reads beside the geometry read

[Ex-2.2.10](/docs/m2/ex-2.2.10/report.py) read the retention drop of ex-2.2.9's `handover` arm off the stored trajectories: the alignment reaches a noisy plateau by epoch 10, and on some seeds drifts slowly down under a constant anchor weight, before the anneal begins. The drift needs the whole-line labeller and the untied readout together; either half alone holds its plateau. Ex-2.2.11 saw it again at fresh seeds (see the notes). What the drift *is*, the trajectories cannot say.

Sandy's reading, from the review of ex-2.2.10: perhaps the model is continuing to search for better representations, and the axis is carried along. The whole-geometry read ([PR #190](https://github.com/z0u/sca2/pull/190): RSA and Procrustes of anchored against control runs) sees a different latent geometry only in the presence of more ops *and* the anchor, which is the same conjunction the drift needs. So the two may be one phenomenon seen from two sides.

The competing reading is a slow slide: the geometry is settled by the peak, and the model walks along a near-flat valley of the task loss where the anchor and the task trade off a little, carried by SGD noise. The two readings predict different things at the stored checkpoints, and the plan below is ordered from the cheapest read that separates them to the most expensive.

## Plan

A stored-checkpoint reanalysis in the mould of the [geometry-rsa report](/docs/m2/geometry-rsa/report.py): no training, no gates, observations. The material is the trajectory checkpoints of ex-2.2.11 (50 points × 3 seeds × 3 anchored conditions, 450 checkpoints; `TRAJ_CHECKPOINT_REF` in its `experiment.py`). Run the per-checkpoint work on Modal: the local memoized backend starts one process per staged task ([eng item](/todo/eng/local-memoized-backend-ignores-workers.md)).

1. **Loss curves, from the trajectories already stored.** Plot the task loss and the anchor loss through the plateau on the three conditions. If `handover`'s task loss keeps falling between the peak and the anneal while the references' is flat, "still searching" has its first support at no cost. Flat on all three favours the slide.
2. **Geometry through training.** The geometry-rsa machinery, run on every trajectory checkpoint: RSA of each checkpoint against its own run's final checkpoint, and against a control's, with the e₁-dropped variant beside. Reorganization predicts the RSA-to-final keeps moving after the alignment peak on `handover` and settles earlier on the references; the slide predicts it is settled by the peak and only the projection onto e₁ moves. The e₁-dropped variant says whether what moves is the rest of the geometry or the axis. This is the direct test, and it is already tooled.
3. **Per-line influence on the anchor, first order.** At each checkpoint, the dot product between each training line's task-loss gradient and the anchor-loss gradient, split by op, by whether the line is red, and by the rule that earned the line its label. A line whose task gradient opposes the anchor gradient is one the anchor loss has to keep correcting. If the non-red lines oppose the anchor on `handover` and not on the references, that is a mechanism for the drift, and the per-line refinement of ex-2.2.12's containment measurement that Sandy asked for. One backward pass per line on a subsample of the corpus, no sampler and no hyperparameters. Take the gradients in the tangent space of the sphere (nGPT re-projects every weight matrix after each step, so the radial component of a gradient is discarded by training). The model is small enough (64-dim, 4 layers) to take the dot products on the fly; if per-line gradients are to be kept for queries chosen later, store a random projection (a sketch: a fixed random matrix, or a sparse count-sketch, down to a few thousand dimensions, which preserves dot products to a small relative error by the Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma; TRAK does this for per-example gradients) rather than the gradients themselves.
4. **The local learning coefficient (LLC)**, as a second signal at the landscape level: an estimate of how many directions in weight space the loss cares about near the current weights, from singular learning theory (Lau et al. 2023, *The Local Learning Coefficient: A Singularity-Aware Complexity Measure*). Developmental interpretability reads a change in the LLC through a loss plateau as a phase transition. Its reading beside step 2, stated in advance: RSA-to-final moving and the LLC rising together is a reorganization; geometry settled and the LLC flat is the slide; a split between them is the interesting case. Three things make this the last step rather than the first. The model lives on a sphere, so the standard SGLD estimator (sampling in unconstrained weight space) would measure a parameterization whose radial directions are exactly flat; the sampler needs the same re-projection after each step, and that nonstandard estimator needs calibrating (chain length, step size, inverse temperature) before a number means anything. The `devinterp` package is PyTorch and the models are Equinox, so it is a port (an SGLD loop in optax is short; the calibration sweep is the work). And a smooth loss of 0.05 in alignment over 25 epochs may show nothing in the LLC, which is evidence too, but a weaker answer than step 2 gives for less. Which loss the estimator sees (the task loss alone, or the task plus the anchor term at that epoch's weight) is a choice to state; through the plateau the anchor weight is constant, so either is defensible.
5. **Bayesian influence (BIF)**, only if step 3 is inconclusive: the covariance, across draws from the SGLD chain of step 4, between a training line's loss and the anchor loss (Kreer et al. 2025, *Bayesian Influence Functions for Hessian-Free Data Attribution*; also in `devinterp`). One query and a subsample of lines make it cheap once the chain exists, and it captures what the first-order read misses. It inherits the sphere caveat.

Budget: about a day for steps 1 to 3; the LLC is its own small piece of work because of the sphere and the port.
